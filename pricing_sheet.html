What we deliver

Managed IT & Support

Keep your team productive. Workstation setup and administration, Microsoft 365 and email, troubleshooting, malware remediation, backup and recovery, and vendor coordination.

Web & App Development

Build a credible digital presence. Custom business websites, client-facing tools and portals, domain and hosting management, SEO foundation, and ongoing maintenance.

Hardware Engineering

Take hardware from concept to product. PCB schematic and layout, embedded firmware, board bring-up and validation — with deep experience in medical device and industrial systems.

Automation & Workflow

Reclaim time from repetitive tasks. Custom scripts and automations, data pipelines, cross-system integrations, and process documentation that reduces manual error at scale.

Data Security & Disposal

Retire old devices without risk. NIST SP 800-88 wipe protocols, certificate of destruction, multi-drive bulk processing, and physical shredding coordination.

Knowledge Transfer

Build your team's capability, not their dependency. System documentation, admin runbooks, walkthrough sessions, and IT continuity planning so your team can operate confidently.
