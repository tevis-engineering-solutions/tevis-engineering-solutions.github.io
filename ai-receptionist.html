AI Receptionist Questions

Will callers know they are talking to an AI?
Yes. It tells every caller it is an AI assistant at the start of the call, and again if anyone asks.
Does it replace my receptionist?
No. It backs up the people you have. It answers when they are busy or gone for the day, and hands every call over with a summary so nothing slips through.
Can it work with my calendar, booking page or CRM?
Yes. Connecting it to the systems you already run is part of the setup we scope and quote with you. It only books into the open times your calendar offers.
Can it answer my existing number?
Yes. It can pick up your existing number when a call goes unanswered, or answer a new number of its own.
Can it sound like us?
Pick any of its ten built-in voices, or have a custom voice made with a voice cloning service such as ElevenLabs, including your own, as a custom setup.
What does it do in an emergency?
It gives your safety instruction first, then gets the caller’s name, number and address and either transfers the call to you or alerts you right away.
What does it cost?
It depends on your business, how many calls you get, and what we connect it to. We quote it after a short call, before any work starts.
What is it built on?
OpenAI’s realtime voice model, hosted on Microsoft Azure. The setup, call handling, safeguards and integrations are built and maintained by Tevis Engineering Solutions in Cleveland.
