Common questions

Can it do more than the three sheds in the demo?
Yes. The demo is a stock shed line because it has to be something. Sizes, wall heights, materials, colors, doors, windows, add-ons, rules and every price are catalog work — we set those up from your own price sheet with no limit on how many you have. A different shape of building, such as a garage, a carport, a cabin or a porch, is development work rather than a catalog edit. We build those too; we scope and quote them up front so the number is honest.
What does it cost?
It is quoted per dealer, because the work is not the same for everyone. Two things drive the number: how large and how custom your catalog is, and where your website is hosted, since some hosts make the install a short job and others take more. You can buy it once and own the build outright with no subscription and no per-lead fee, or have us host and maintain it and handle your price changes for you. Ask for a quote and you will get a fixed number.
Who changes the prices when my costs go up?
You do, and it does not involve us. Your styles, sizes, colors, options, prices and rules live in a small set of plain files on your own host. Edit a price, save, and the next customer sees the new number — no rebuild, no deploy, no support ticket. A validator is included that checks the whole price sheet before you publish and tells you in plain English if something does not line up, such as a size you advertise but never priced.
Will it work on my existing website?
In almost every case, yes. It runs on ordinary web hosting — WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Shopify, Webflow, GoDaddy and custom sites included — and it lives on your own domain, typically at a page like yoursite.com/build. We handle the install rather than emailing you a code snippet and wishing you luck.
Is the price the customer sees a real quote?
It is an estimate, and the builder says so on screen. It prices the building from your own price sheet, but it does not know the delivery distance to that address, the site prep the lot needs, or local permitting — so every path ends with you confirming the number. That is deliberate. The tool exists to start a real conversation with someone who has already picked a building and seen a payment.
What about the rent-to-own payment it shows?
It is a display, not a credit offer. It is calculated from payment factors you enter in your own price sheet, so it mirrors the way your rent-to-own provider quotes. Nothing is submitted to a lender, no application is taken, and no approval is implied. If you do not sell rent-to-own, remove the terms and the builder shows the cash price alone.
Does the 3D model show every option I sell?
No, and that is on purpose. Doors, windows, sizes, wall heights, materials and colors are drawn on the building. Add-ons such as insulation, electrical packages, shelving, ramps and anchor kits are priced and itemized but not modeled, because drawing them adds nothing to a customer's decision. Everything selected appears in the itemized price whether it is drawn or not.
