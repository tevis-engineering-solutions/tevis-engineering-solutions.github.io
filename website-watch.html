Frequently Asked Questions

Is the first month really free?
Yes. PayPal shows the free month on its approval page, and the first charge comes a month after you sign up. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. The free month is for a business's first time with Website Watch, so signing up again for the same site or email starts at the regular price.
What counts as down?
No answer within 15 seconds, a server error, or the home page being gone, on two checks in a row. A site that answers but turns us away, which is how some firewalls behave, counts as up.
Does it change anything on my website?
No. Each check loads your home page the way a visitor's browser would and closes the connection. Nothing is installed, and we never log in or submit anything.
Where do the renewal dates come from?
The domain date comes from your registrar's public registration record. The certificate date comes from the public certificate logs that every certificate authority publishes to. Both are checked once a day.
Do you need my passwords?
Never. Website Watch looks only at what the public can see. For Insights you add us as a read-only user in Google, or create a read-only key in Shopify or Cloudflare; we send the steps and a secure link, store keys encrypted, and you can remove our access at any time.
What is the visitor line, and is it private?
One short script tag we give you (or add for you). It counts page views and clicks on your phone number, email, forms and buy buttons. It sets no cookies and keeps no personal data, so you do not need a cookie banner for it.
What do you check on my Google listing?
Every week: new 1 and 2 star reviews, whether your rating fell under 4.0, and whether the phone number, hours, address and website link on Google match your website, or Google shows you as closed. Every month: how your reviews and rating compare with businesses like yours nearby. The week before major holidays, we check whether your holiday hours are set on Google and remind you if they are not. When a bad review comes in, the alert includes a calm reply drafted for you to post or edit. Your portal also has your review link, a QR code, and a way to email review requests to up to 100 customers a month. We only read your public listing; we never post or change anything on it.
Will you tell me where I show up in search and in AI answers?
Yes. We suggest 3 to 5 phrases your customers search, like “roofer Parma OH”, and you can change them. Every month the report card shows where your site lands in the search results for each, and whether it moved. We also ask an AI assistant the question your customers ask (“Who are the best roofers in Parma?”) and tell you whether you were named. If you were not, we say the usual reasons: fewer reviews than nearby businesses, a phone or address that does not match across the web, or a thin website.
Can you check that my contact form still works?
Yes, on request. We find your contact form, note which service sends it (Formspree, WPForms, Jotform, HubSpot and others), and check every month that the form and the service behind it still answer. We never submit it, so no test messages land in your inbox. If it breaks, you get one email. Your report card also tracks your speed and accessibility scores month to month.
How would you know if my site was hacked?
Every day we read your home page and a couple of your key pages the way a visitor does, and once more the way Google's crawler does. We look for planted spam links and text (pills, casinos, loans), links hidden from visitors, a page that shows Google something different, and Google's own danger list. If we find something, you hear the same day, in plain words, with what to do next.
Do you check the software my site runs on?
Yes, from the outside. Every day we compare the WordPress plugins and themes, and the script libraries like jQuery, that your site shows publicly against public lists of known security holes. When one has a fix, you get a short email naming it and the version to update to. Once a week we also list your tracking tags (Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta and others), check Google's Consent Mode, and note slow outside widgets and accessibility overlays.
Will you tell me if my email gets blacklisted?
Yes. Every day we check the servers that send your email against the public spam blacklists. If one gets listed, you get one email with the link to ask for removal, and another when it clears. Servers run by Google, Microsoft and other big providers are theirs to manage, so we name them rather than alert you.
What if someone copies my website?
We watch the public certificate logs for new web addresses that look like yours. When one shows a page imitating your business (your name, phone number or wording), we report it to Google and email you when we do. Parked and unrelated pages are left alone.
How do I cancel?
In PayPal, under your automatic payments, or from the Billing page in your customer portal. Watching continues until the end of the period you paid for.
Can you watch more than one site?
Yes. Each site is its own subscription, so subscribe once per site. If you have several, get in touch and we will set them up together.
