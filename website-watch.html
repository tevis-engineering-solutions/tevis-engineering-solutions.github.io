    Questions

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the first month really free?
    Yes. PayPal shows the free month on its approval page, and the first charge comes a month after you sign up. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. The free month is for a business's first time with Website Watch, so signing up again for the same site or email starts at the regular price.
    What counts as down?
    No answer within 15 seconds, a server error, or the home page being gone, on two checks in a row. A site that answers but turns us away, which is how some firewalls behave, counts as up.
    Does it change anything on my website?
    No. Each check loads your home page the way a visitor's browser would and closes the connection. Nothing is installed, and we never log in or submit anything.
    Where do the renewal dates come from?
    The domain date comes from your registrar's public registration record. The certificate date comes from the public certificate logs that every certificate authority publishes to. Both are checked once a day.
    How do I cancel?
    In PayPal, under your automatic payments, or from the Billing page in your customer portal. Watching continues until the end of the period you paid for.
    Can you watch more than one site?
    Yes. Each site is its own subscription, so subscribe once per site. If you have several, get in touch and we will set them up together.
