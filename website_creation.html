Frequently Asked Questions

Is the website analysis really free?
Yes. No email address, no account, no sales call attached to it. We run the audit, you get the report, and the findings are yours whether or not you ever hire us.
How much does a website cost?
It depends on what it needs to do. A simple site is a very different job from a store or a customer portal. We scope every project up front and quote it before any work begins. Our guide on small business website costs walks through realistic ranges.
Can you take over a website someone else built?
Yes. We start with a one-time review to learn how it was built and hosted, then we can maintain it or rebuild it. Running the free analysis first is a good way to see what you are dealing with.
Do you build the logins, payments, and tools shown above, or just the website?
Both. The customer portal, billing, ticketing, and free tools on this site were built in-house and run every day. If your project needs any of them, we have already built and shipped that piece.
Do you handle hosting, domains, and email?
Yes. We set up and manage hosting, your domain, and professional email on your own domain. Ownership stays in your name.
