What moves a project from simple to complex

Simple

A single page, or three to five pages. Light custom design, a contact form that reaches you, and the SEO basics done right.

Moderate

Five to fifteen pages, custom design, booking or quote forms, SEO work, and a blog or simple content manager.

Complex

Custom functionality: e-commerce, customer accounts, payment processing, live tools on the page, and third-party integrations.

Very Complex

A database-driven platform with sign-in, billing, ticketing, automated email, scheduled jobs, and a staff console. The portal behind this site is one of these.

What drives cost up: user accounts and authentication, payment and transaction processing, database design, custom integrations with your CRM or accounting, inventory management, multi-language support, custom analytics, role-based content control, and real-time notifications. We have built every one of these for ourselves, so the estimate comes from experience. We tell you which of them you actually need, and which ones you have been sold before.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the website analysis really free?
Yes. No email address, no account, no sales call attached to it. We run the audit, you get the report, and the findings are yours whether or not you ever hire us. It is the same tool we run on prospects before we contact them, so showing it to you costs us nothing we were not already spending.
What does the scanner actually look at?
It reads your homepage and a couple of linked pages the way a browser does, then runs 28 objective checks across six categories: revenue-blocking defects, trust problems, staleness, local-SEO invisibility, polish, and technical health. It also asks Google to measure your site's speed on a phone. It does not log in anywhere, change anything, or store your pages.
How much does a website cost?
It depends on scope. A simple brochure site is a very different job from a booking or online-store build. We scope and quote every project up front, and our blog guide on small business website costs walks through realistic ranges.
Do you offer ongoing website maintenance?
Yes. Month-to-month care plans covering updates, backups, security, and small changes, starting at $79 per month, with a loyalty discount for sites we built. Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice.
Will my site work on phones and show up on Google?
Yes. Every site is mobile-responsive and built with SEO fundamentals so it loads fast and can be found and indexed.
Can you take over a website someone else built?
Yes. We start with a one-time discovery and onboarding step to learn the existing setup, then we can maintain it or rebuild it. Running the free analysis first is a good way to see what you are dealing with.
Do you build web apps and customer portals, or just websites?
Both. The customer portal behind the Login button on this site, the billing and ticketing underneath it, the subscription software we sell, and the free tools on our product pages were all built in-house and run in production every day. If your project needs sign-in, payments, automated email, a staff console, or a live tool on the page, we have already built and shipped each of those pieces.
Do you use templates or page builders?
Only when a hosted page builder is genuinely the right fit for your budget and needs, and we will say so. This site, and everything behind it, is hand-written rather than assembled in a page builder, so it loads fast, passes validation, and can grow into a portal or a store later without starting over.
Do you handle hosting, domains, and email?
Yes. We can set up and manage hosting, your domain, and professional email on your own domain. Ownership stays in your name, which is not true everywhere.
