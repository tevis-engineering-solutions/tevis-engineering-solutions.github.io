Who we are

About Tevis Engineering

Founded by Tyler Tevis, an Air Force Veteran and professional computer engineer with a successful career in research and 7 years of experience in engineering design and IT support, Tevis Engineering Solutions is a versatile technology-forward company dedicated to providing elite services. With such extensive experience in prototype design across engineering disciplines, solving complex problems is second nature.

Providing reliable local and remote IT support, engineering and tangential solutions in the Cleveland area, Tevis Engineering Solutions offers scaled-to-fit solutions that don't break the bank. We deliver military-grade precision along with practical, actionable consulting and 3D printing solutions. Truly a one-stop-shop for your technology needs at an affordable rate.

Contact us to see how we can improve antiquated processes, expand your network, deploy those workstations and/or harness the technology that's out there to take your productivity to the next level — or restore it to its former glory!
