IT Support Built for Solon Businesses

Solon has an unusually strong manufacturing and corporate base for a suburb its size. Its business parks along Aurora Road, Solon Road and Cochran Road hold manufacturers, engineering firms, distributors and technology companies, alongside medical offices, professional services and retail.

Smaller firms there often work beside much larger neighbors and are held to similar expectations by their customers: secure email and file sharing, dependable backups, controlled access to shared data, and networks that keep production and office systems running. That's the gap we fill — enterprise-grade IT practices, sized and priced for a Solon small business. And when a project needs engineering rather than IT, we do that too.

Solon IT Support — Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer on-site IT support in Solon, Ohio?
Yes. We provide both on-site and remote IT support to businesses throughout Solon. Our office is in the Cleveland area, so an on-site visit to Solon is a short drive, and most issues that don't require hands-on work are resolved remotely the same day.
What size businesses in Solon do you work with?
We focus on solo practices, home-based businesses, and small teams up to roughly 50 employees — exactly the kind of business that needs reliable IT but can't justify a full-time internal IT department.
How quickly can you respond to a Solon business?
Standard response is within one business day. Managed-plan clients get priority response, and many remote issues are handled same-day. For a business-down emergency we do everything we can to get you working again as fast as possible.
What does IT support cost for a small business in Solon?
You can work with us on a flat-rate monthly managed plan or on an as-needed hourly basis (standard engineering rate is $80/hour). We scope and quote every engagement up front, so there are no surprise bills. See the pricing sheet for details.
