Capture bookmark (OhioBuys, BidNet)

For the sites the queue cannot read on its own: OhioBuys, which checks for a browser, and BidNet. The bookmark runs in your own signed-in tab, reads what that site is showing you, and hands it to this console.

BidNet’s terms bar reproducing its listings electronically; this keeps a private copy for your own evaluation, read at a person’s pace.

TES capture

Using the capture bookmark

  1. Once: show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B) and drag the gold TES capture button onto it. Drag it again after an update here, since the bookmark holds its own copy of the code.
  2. Open the OhioBuys solicitation list (View Public Solicitations) or the BidNet Ohio open solicitations list, and click TES capture on the bar. Allow pop-ups if Chrome asks.
  3. A new tab opens on this console and waits. The site’s tab shows a small progress box: OhioBuys takes about 10 seconds and stops at the first page with nothing open; BidNet about a minute and a half.
  4. In the new tab, click Add N to the queue. Closed posts are left out, and a post that is on both sites is kept once, with its OhioBuys link.

On a single OhioBuys post, the same click also reads its documents and offers Build a bid with the documents.
