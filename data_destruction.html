Questions

Before you hand over your drives

What methods do you use?
The one NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1 calls for on that media and for what happens to it next: clear or purge when the drive will be reused, destroy when it will not. Hard drives and SSDs are handled differently, because a method that works on one does not always work on the other.
Do I get a certificate of destruction?
Yes, with any job that includes one, along with the chain-of-custody log. It lists the date, the method and each drive’s serial number, so it works for audits and your own records.
Does this help with HIPAA or other compliance?
The records line up with what HIPAA, GLBA, FERPA and PCI DSS expect for disposing of media. Your exact obligations are best confirmed with your own compliance or legal advisor; we provide the work and the evidence.
What can you handle, and where?
Hard drives, SSDs and other storage from desktops, laptops, servers and backup systems. We work on-site across Cleveland and Northeast Ohio, or pick the drives up on a scheduled date.
Are you third-party certified?
No, and we will not claim to be until we are. What you get is a documented process that follows NIST SP 800-88, and a record of every drive you can show anyone who asks.
